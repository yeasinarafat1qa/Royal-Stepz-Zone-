Qatar Delivery Rates & Zones

We provide express footwear delivery across all major municipalities and zones in Qatar:

  • Doha Municipality: Free Delivery on orders above QAR 200, otherwise QAR 15. Delivery within 24 Hours.
  • Al Wakrah & Al Wukair: QAR 20 standard delivery charge. Delivery within 24-48 Hours.
  • Lusail & The Pearl Qatar: QAR 15 delivery charge. Express delivery available.
  • Al Rayyan & Umm Salal: QAR 20 delivery charge. Delivery within 48 Hours.

Returns & Replacements Policy

At Royal Stepz Zone, we ensure hassle-free returns and size replacements in Qatar:

  • 7-Day Return Window: You can request a return or size exchange within 7 days of delivery.
  • Condition: Footwear must be unworn, in original condition, and with the box/tags intact.
  • How to Initiate: Use your "My Orders" section to edit or cancel, or contact WhatsApp support.
